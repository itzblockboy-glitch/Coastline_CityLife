1.5 · City life & police

  • Four new civilian base models, 24 distinct outfit/palette combinations, varied height and thin-to-chubby body widths. No civilian uses the player model.
  • Separate licensed Sketchfab police character, 4 street officers, patrol cars and escalating responders.
  • 1 star: attacked civilian fights back lightly. 2 stars: 4 response officers plus street police, melee only. 3 stars: 8 response officers with firearms. 4 stars: 12. 5 stars: 20 response officers plus street officers, faster attacks and heavier automatic weapons.
  • Armed attacked civilians can shoot back at 3+ stars; not every bystander attacks.
  • Up to 2 patrol and 5 response cruisers with road-grid navigation, flashing light effects and siren.
  • Existing car fleet replaced with licensed Sketchfab models; motorcycle retained from its licensed Sketchfab source. Meter-based vehicle sizes and distinct individual top speeds.
  • Distinct procedural engine signatures, sidearm/carbine/heavy-gun and punch sounds. SFX volume in Settings. No streamed samples.
  • Camera button or desktop V cycles third-person, first-person and front-facing second-person views. Also in Settings.
  • High graphics is now default. Reduce to Balanced/Low if your device heats up.

Limits

Original compact city, not GTA V/VI. Police navigation is simple collision-aware grid pursuit, not a production driving/traffic AI; it can get stuck. AI/combat remain local, not synchronized multiplayer combat. Sounds are synthesized profiles, not recordings of real engines. High quality and a 5-star crowd cost more GPU time; physical Android FPS/heat and microphone routing have not been verified.
