Coastline 1.3 · Stability update

Mobile-focused release, tuned conservatively for the user's requested mid-range / 6–8 GB device class. Physical phone performance remains unverified.

Changes in this release

  • Corrected camera-relative left/right joystick and keyboard movement.
  • Motorcycle mesh forward axis corrected; rider now faces the vehicle's travel direction. Wrist/limb bone lookup handles the loader's punctuation removal.
  • Heading-up rotating minimap: world rotates with actor heading, player chevron stays upright. No ±π interpolation flip.
  • Walking/running, punch and jump animation states; upper-body firing overlay, visible recoil and muzzle flash on both weapons.
  • Timed car entry/exit and bike mount/dismount using the existing Interact clip and procedural position paths. These are lightweight interaction animations, not bespoke motion-captured door/seat animations.
  • Weapons attached to the actual right-wrist bone with calibrated grip orientation/scale; no longer fixed to the world/player torso.
  • One-time indexed-skinned-mesh measurement, fixed unanimated sizing wrapper, sanitized quaternion-only clips and normalization invariants. No animation is permitted to rescale or translate the normalized rig.
  • Tree trunks excluded from road/intersection footprints; 107 solid trunk colliders in the current seeded city. Building collision tests, oriented vehicle footprints, circle/box narrow-phase tests and short movement substeps. Traffic stops when blocked.
  • 24 animated civilians. Ten pooled officer slots are activated progressively: two at one star with melee only; four at two stars with firearms; six/eight/ten at three/four/five stars. Pursuit speed, firearm cadence and damage increase. Health, busted respawn and wanted escape/decay.
  • Accelerator/brake captions removed; pedal icons and accessibility labels remain. Safer spawn reset, transition cancellation and low-speed exit checks.

Police & controls

Assaulting a civilian raises one star. Public weapon firing raises at least two; further offenses escalate. At one star officers pursue and punch, never shoot. From two stars they can fire when in line of sight and range. Escape their sight/range and stop offending to lose wanted levels. At zero health the player respawns. Police behavior is a basic local pursuit/avoidance system, not a full navigation mesh, traffic police or GTA AI recreation.

On foot: joystick, drag to look, Jump / Sprint / Punch or Fire. Driving: steering arrows, icon-only accelerator and brake/reverse pedals, center exit button. Slow below approximately 14 km/h before exiting. Garage selection is unavailable during interaction transitions. Minimap expands on tap. Weapon button cycles fists/sidearm/carbine; ammo tap reloads.

Checks performed

The browser regression harness checks joystick/keyboard direction, changing walk-leg rotation, punch/jump states, firing overlay, car entry/exit, three bike mount/dismount cycles, fixed scale and measured standing height near 1.85 m, zero trunks on road footprints, collision predicates, wrist attachment, both weapons, and the one/two-star police split (melee damage versus firearm shots). Exact results are included with the source. These are not exhaustive tests and do not certify a bug-free game.

What is not part of 1.3

As selected by the user, this release prioritizes mobile stability over an asset overhaul. It does not add the requested 20 cars + 20 bikes, 40 unique recorded engine sounds, a realistic replacement protagonist, a photorealistic world or GTA V/VI maps. The existing licensed assets and original compact city remain. No redistributed Rockstar map/assets are included.

Solo play is offline. Rooms/voice still require a shared HTTPS backend; some networks need TURN for voice. Police/civilian AI, wanted levels and combat are local, not synchronized multiplayer state. Vehicle ownership/physics are not server-authoritative. Remote vehicle teleportation/network contention may still occur. No claim of completely fixed collisions under every network/physics situation or guaranteed 60 FPS.
