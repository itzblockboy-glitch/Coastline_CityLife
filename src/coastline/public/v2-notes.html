Coastline 1.2

Mobile sandbox update · Original city · Android package ai.arena.coastline

Cleaner controls & screen

Removed the promotional headline, discovery panel, health decoration, desktop hints and status clutter from gameplay. The corrected north-fixed minimap has a proper heading arrow, high-DPI canvas, room-player markers and tap-to-expand view. On foot use the left joystick and drag right to look. Driving uses separate left/right steering buttons and right-side ACCELERATOR and BRAKE / REVERSE pedals. Hold brake to stop, then keep holding to reverse. Exit is at bottom center.

Tap the garage icon to bring a vehicle to a nearby open street. The weapon button cycles fists, Pulse sidearm and Carbine; hold the action button to fire, tap ammo to reload. These are fictional Kenney Blaster Kit models, not replicas of real firearms. Shots use local raycasts and non-graphic pedestrian hit reactions; weapons are not network-synchronized and do not damage remote players.

Visual & audio changes

Photographic asphalt/concrete textures with mipmaps and normal maps, HDR sky and reflections, neutral/desaturated building colors, fixed missing commercial building textures, softened shadows, PBR concept cars and motorcycles. This remains a mixed-detail mobile city: buildings, palms and protagonist still use simplified stylized geometry. It is not a photorealistic or GTA V-quality renderer.

Original relaxing 68 BPM ambient pads and soft plucked notes, generated entirely on-device with Web Audio. No streaming, samples, internet audio or music royalties. Volume and mute are saved. Music and microphone pause in the background.

Important limits

Android 8+ and current Android System WebView with WebGL2. APK is development/self-signed, not Play Store certified. Physical-device installation, real microphone routing, battery/thermal behavior and extended sessions are not verified. 60 FPS is not guaranteed.

Offline solo play needs no internet. Rooms/voice need a shared reachable HTTPS server entered in Play with friends. No permanent public server is bundled. Voice uses STUN; restrictive networks may require TURN. Multiplayer remains client-led: no authoritative vehicle ownership/physics, anti-cheat, shared mission progress, synchronized traffic/NPC AI or multiplayer weapon combat. Bike riding is arcade-style with a simple posed rider, not a motorbike simulator.
