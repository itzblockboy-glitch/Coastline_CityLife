1.4 · Realistic-asset update

  • New textured Rocketbox human and biped-compatible animation set.
  • All 128 buildings replaced with six photo-textured modular apartment/factory variants.
  • Textured palms, street lamps and pole collision.
  • Near/far building detail, spatial instancing and reduced texture sizes.
  • Previous controls, bike mounting, collisions, rotating minimap, escalating police, offline play, multiplayer and voice retained.

What this is not

Not a GTA V/VI map, a photorealistic recreation, or a 40-vehicle expansion. The compact grid and existing vehicle fleet remain. Physical-phone FPS, heat and native microphone behavior are not certified. Low quality reduces shadows and rendering cost.
