Coastline 1.5

An original compact coastal sandbox. Not a Rockstar product or a GTA V/VI map.

Limitations and networking

This is a mobile WebGL prototype, not photorealistic GTA-scale graphics. All city/building models are external, licensed assets assembled into an original map; road/terrain surfaces, ocean, UI and effects are generated. Licensed generic vehicle models and fictional weapons remain mixed-detail assets. No interiors, full vehicle physics or streaming world. FPS and heat are not verified on a physical phone.

Solo assets and music work offline. Multiplayer/voice require a reachable server, HTTPS and microphone permission. Rooms are not authentication; use with trusted friends. STUN-only voice can fail on cellular networks without a TURN relay. Voice is not recorded. Client-led movement is not authoritative or cheat-resistant.
